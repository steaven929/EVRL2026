Key Insights

1. Charging Reward Signal Strength:
   - Each charging vehicle contributes: 50 reward points
   - Total charging contribution: 1950.00 (31.00% of total)
   - Average reward per step: 524.17
   - Charging reward is 0.47x average step reward

2. Why Actor Doesn't Learn to Charge:
   a) Reward Signal Dilution:
      - Charging reward: 1950.00
      - Service revenue (estimated): 4340.00
      - Charging is only 31.00% of total reward

   b) Temporal Credit Assignment:
      - Charging benefit is long-term (prevents future SOC penalty)
      - But gamma=0.95 may not be high enough
      - Immediate service revenue dominates learning

   c) Exploration Dependency:
      - Actor outputs ~10^-6 for charging preferences
      - Only noise (+/-0.3) triggers charging
      - Without noise, no charging → no learning signal

3. Proposed Solutions:
   A. Increase charging reward:
      - Current: 500 per vehicle (50 after /10)
      - Proposed: 1000-2000 per vehicle (100-200 after /10)
      - Make charging reward comparable to service revenue

   B. Lower charging threshold:
      - Current: 0.1 (too high for Actor's 10^-6 output)
      - Proposed: 0.01 or 0.001
      - Allow Actor's small outputs to trigger charging

   C. Extend exploration period:
      - Current: 20 epochs with charging noise
      - Proposed: 50-100 epochs
      - Give Actor more time to learn from charging experiences

   D. Increase gamma:
      - Current: 0.95
      - Proposed: 0.98 or 0.99
      - Strengthen long-term reward signal
